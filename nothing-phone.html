One dot.
More possibilities.

OneGlyph is a utility project for the single Glyph light on the Nothing Phone (3a) Lite. It explores small interactions that make the rear light useful beyond decoration.

OneGlyph / v2.0

Bringing the Glyph to life.

Music reactions, notification cues, Morse code, timers, a metronome, and charging feedback — with compact tools designed around the phone's single Glyph dot.
